Strengthen your secure design skills with this intensive, hands-on Threat Modeling with STRIDE Bootcamp.


Across 2 days, you'll learn to uncover threats early, design safer systems, and build the mindset of a security architect.


This bootcamp is designed for developers, architects, security analysts, and anyone responsible for building or evaluating secure systems.


 
 

What You'll Learn:


  • Threat Modeling Foundations: Understand the fundamentals of threat modeling and why it's critical to secure design.
  • Apply STRIDE Effectively: Use Microsoft's STRIDE methodology to systematically identify threats across applications and systems.
  • Data Flow Diagrams (DFDs): Create accurate DFDs, identify trust boundaries, and analyze complex system interactions.
  • Structured Threat Analysis:
    • Conduct guided threat modeling sessions across real-world architectures.
  • Mitigation Strategy Development: Prioritize threats and build actionable mitigation plans based on risk impact.
  • Documentation & Reporting: Learn to document findings for audits, stakeholders, and compliance needs.
